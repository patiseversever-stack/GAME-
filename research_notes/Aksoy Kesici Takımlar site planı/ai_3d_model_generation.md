## Q1. Which AI 3D generators are strongest in 2026 for sharp-edged, hard-surface, metallic objects (inputs, outputs, quality, pricing, licensing)?

### Takeaway
The current leaders for hard-surface work are **Hyper3D Rodin Gen-2.5** (May 2026), **Meshy 7 / 7.1** (Aug/Sep 2026) plus Meshy 6, **Tripo H3.1 / v3.x / Smart Mesh P1.0**, and **Tencent Hunyuan 3D 3.0/3.1 (hosted "Pro")**. Benchmarks disagree. On one arena Hunyuan 3D Pro leads on both looks and mesh cleanliness, with Rodin a close second on looks. On another arena Tripo v3.1 leads. For commercial use the deciding factor is licensing. **Rodin** gives commercial rights on all tiers (pay per download, previews free). **Meshy and Tripo** require a paid plan for an attribution-free or commercial license: Meshy Free is CC BY 4.0 and Tripo Free is non-commercial. Hunyuan's hosted-platform commercial terms could not be verified. **TRELLIS.2 (MIT)** is the only fully free, commercially safe option, but it needs a GPU or a Hugging Face Space. Text-to-CAD tools (Zoo, Adam, Backflip) output real CAD/STEP but are not suited to producing a photoreal hero asset of a complex tool.

## Q2. Which candidate product is easiest for AI to reproduce with high fidelity?

### Takeaway
Rank for AI fidelity, from easiest to hardest: (4) single insert > **(2) PCLNR holder + CNMG insert** > (1) MGEHR grooving holder > (3) drill / end mill. The best AI hero is **(2) the PCLNR external turning holder with a CNMG insert**. It has a chunky prismatic shank, a large flat rhombic insert, a recognizable silhouette and no thin spirals. The drill/end mill is the worst AI candidate because of thin helical flutes, but it is the **best candidate for procedural/code modeling**. Tiny laser-etched text and logos will not come out as geometry with any tool. They should be added later as a decal/texture or dropped.

### Cited Findings
- Industrial text-to-3D research (ForgeDreamer, arXiv 2603.09266) reports that AI-generated images "failed to accurately represent fine details like spiral grooves," and in some cases generated "shapes that lacked characteristic geometric features" — [ForgeDreamer paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.09266).
- General text-to-3D tools "don't produce extruded lettering"; logos and 3D text need dedicated tools — [3D AI Studio text-to-3D page (search summary)](https://www.3daistudio.com/TextTo3D).
- Prompt guides for hard-surface items advise "simple geometry," "no small greebles," "avoid thin elements," "thick supports," and "readable silhouette from distance." This implies thin features are a known failure mode — [Rephrase-it Meshy/Tripo prompt guide](https://rephrase-it.com/blog/prompts-for-ai-3d-generation-that-actually-work-meshy-tripo-); [Tripo prompt engineering](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/text-to-3d-prompt-engineering).
- Meshy 6 is described as producing "sharper edges, clearer silhouettes… Bolts read as bolts, panels read as panels" for mechanical parts. This suits prismatic holders and clamps — [Toolworthy Meshy v6](https://www.toolworthy.ai/tool/meshy-ai-v6).
- Meshy 7's alignment claim is strongest on overall proportion (81.0%) and weakest on surface details (59.8%). Coarse shape is reproduced better than fine detail — [3D Printing Industry](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/meshy-launches-meshy-7-image-to-3d-model-built-around-alignment-253786/).
- Reference-image guidance notes AI struggles with transparent or reflective materials ("Glass or transparent plastic is difficult") and that heavy shadows get interpreted as geometry — [Sloyd reference-image guide](https://www.sloyd.ai/blog/turning-reference-images-into-3d-models). Photogrammetry-style methods "suffer from high failure rates on thin or reflective surfaces" — [Neural4D image-to-3D page](https://www.neural4d.com/features/ai-generated-3d-models-from-image).

### Inferences
- **(2) PCLNR + CNMG (recommended for AI).** The shank is a simple box (e.g., 20×20 or 25×25 mm). The head is a short angled block. The CNMG insert is a large flat 80° rhombus with a center hole. The clamp (lever or top clamp + screw) is chunky. Every feature is several millimetres thick, so it suits volumetric generators (Sparc3D/TRELLIS/Hunyuan voxel resolutions of ~1024–1536³ resolve features down to roughly 1/1000 of object size). It is also the "icon" of turning tooling and reads well while rotating. The insert contrasts in colour (gold/black coated carbide vs dark steel), which helps both AI segmentation and a later material swap. PCLNR is a standardized ISO designation made by every brand (Iscar, Korloy, Tungaloy, Kyocera and others), so manufacturer STEP is easy to find as a fallback (see Q6).
- **(1) MGEHR1616-2 grooving holder (good, slightly riskier).** The square shank (16×16 mm) and top clamp screw are easy. The risk is the narrow **blade-like head**: per the designation, the "-2" means a ~2 mm insert width, so the blade and insert pocket are only ~2 mm thick. AI generators tend to thicken, fuse or melt features that are thin relative to object length (~100–110 mm). Laser-etched text will be lost. Expect the insert to fuse into the blade with no visible seat.
- **(3) Drill / end mill (avoid for AI; ideal procedurally).** Helical flutes are thin, twisted, deep concave features with self-occlusion. The ForgeDreamer evidence shows AI fails exactly here, and coolant holes are sub-millimetre at scale. An end mill, however, is mathematically simple (a 4-lobe cross-section swept along a helix plus a cylindrical shank). It can be generated perfectly in three.js or Blender Python, with correct metal/AlTiN materials and no AI artifacts. A procedural end mill spinning about its axis on scroll is also a very strong, lightweight hero.
- **(4) Single insert.** It is easiest geometrically, and AI or code can both do it trivially. But it is small, flat and visually weak as a rotating hero. Best used as a secondary element, e.g., an insert that "flies out" of the holder.
- Weighing fidelity and hero appeal together gives this order: **PCLNR+CNMG** (AI) ≈ **procedural end mill** (code) > MGEHR (AI) > insert alone.

## Q3. Prompt engineering: text-to-3D prompt templates, negative prompts, and reference-image preparation (incl. which AI image generator to use)

### Takeaway
For metal tools, **image-to-3D beats text-to-3D**. The best workflow is to create (or photograph) one clean, high-resolution reference on a pure white background, at a three-quarter view, with soft even lighting, a matte/satin finish (no mirror reflections), and no text or logos. Optionally add 2–3 consistent extra views (front/side/top) for multi-view modes, which need Meshy Pro (Meshy 7) or Tripo multi-view, or use Hunyuan 3.1 with up to 8 views. Meshy has **no negative-prompt field**: write exclusions inside the main prompt ("no text, no base, no background"). Describe *geometry* and *materials* separately.

### Cited Findings
- Prompt structure: separate "what it is" from "how it should be built." Describe one object, not a scene. For hard surface use "clean mesh, simple silhouette, no tiny floating parts, symmetrical geometry." Prompt "smooth, polished surface geometry suitable for a metallic material" rather than "shiny chrome" to get a clean mesh for later PBR — [Rephrase-it prompt guide](https://rephrase-it.com/blog/prompts-for-ai-3d-generation-that-actually-work-meshy-tripo-); [Meshy prompting guide](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/guides/prompting); [Meshy 3D prompt guide 2026](https://www.meshy.ai/tutorials/3d-prompt-guide); [Meshy prompt templates](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/16103167-meshy-prompt-templates-for-3d-assets).
- **Meshy 5 and Meshy 6 do not support negative prompting.** Put exclusions in the main prompt, e.g., "no background elements, without floating particles, no text or labels" — [Meshy help: negative prompt](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/9992028-how-to-use-negative-prompt-for-text-to-3d-i-can-t-find-any-text-input-to-enter-one).
- Tripo: explicitly exclude unwanted features ("no thin sections, no complex overhangs"). Iterate by changing one block (shape vs materials) at a time — [Tripo prompt engineering](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/text-to-3d-prompt-engineering); [Tripo 25 image-to-3D tips](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/25-image-to-3d-prompt-tips-for-cleaner-ai-3d-models).

### Inferences
- **Best reference source: a real photo of the actual product.** Aksoy physically sells these tools. ## Q4. Best export settings for web (GLB, PBR, polycount, textures, quads vs tris, symmetry, origin, axis, scale)

### Takeaway
Ask the user to export **GLB with PBR (metallic-roughness) textures at 2K**, at the **highest geometry quality the tool offers**: triangle/"raw" high tier (Rodin), or Meshy/Tripo's default high-detail result. Do **not** ask for an aggressive in-tool reduction. Quad remesh is only useful for animation or sculpting, not for a static scroll-rotated mesh. The developer then decimates to roughly 30k–80k triangles, compresses, recentres, rescales and reorients in the container (Q5). If the tool exposes them, origin = center/bottom and real-world height can be set (Meshy remesh `origin_at`, `resize_height`).

### Cited Findings
- Meshy remesh: quad-dominant or triangle; target polycount 100–300,000 (default 30,000); `origin_at: "bottom"`; `resize_height` (e.g., 1.0) for real-world size. - Rodin Gen-2.5: Quad 4K–50K faces vs Triangle up to 500K. Quad is meant "for clean animation rigs," triangle for maximum detail/sculpts. There is also a "Smart Low-Poly" mode for real-time — [3D AI Studio Rodin 2.5 tutorial](https://www.3daistudio.com/blog/how-to-use-rodin-gen-2-5-online-tutorial); [Scenario Rodin 2.5](https://www.scenario.com/models/rodin-gen-25).
- Tripo exports GLB (default), OBJ, FBX, USD, STL, 3MF with baked PBR color/roughness/metalness — [Tripo image-to-3D page (search summary)](https://www.tripo3d.ai/features/image-to-3d-model). ### Inferences
- **What to tell the user to click** (generic across tools):
  - Format: **GLB** (not FBX/OBJ: GLB packs textures in one file).
  - Textures/material: **PBR** ("PBR" in Rodin; Meshy/Tripo default PBR). Resolution **2K** (2048). 4K is unnecessary; we resize anyway.
  - Geometry: **highest-detail triangle mesh** (Rodin: "Triangle/Raw" at Medium–High tier; Meshy: do not remesh, or remesh to ~100k triangles; Tripo: default/HD result). If the user can only choose quads, accept Rodin Quad at the high end (~50K).
  - Symmetry: **off** (right-hand holders are asymmetric).
  - Origin/scale/axis: leave defaults. Most tools export correctly, but AI models arrive at arbitrary scale (often normalized to ~1–2 units).

One 2K base-colour, plus optionally a 1K–2K normal map, with metal/roughness mostly replaced by procedural material values. Compress with meshopt or Draco. Textures as WebP (smallest download) or KTX2 (lowest GPU memory). Aim for a final GLB around ≤2–4 MB for a hero that must load fast on mobile.
- Ask the user to also download the **untextured/"white model"** if the tool offers one (or the highest-poly version). Clean geometry matters more than baked textures, which we plan to replace.

## Q5. Post-processing the developer can do in a Linux container (gltf-transform, material replacement, splitting parts for an exploded animation, Blender headless)

### Takeaway
Everything after download can be automated. Use `gltf-transform` (weld/simplify/compress/resize/center), replace AI-baked materials in three.js with clean `MeshPhysicalMaterial` metal, and use Blender headless or trimesh for reorientation and splitting. An **exploded holder/insert animation is not reliably possible from a single fused AI mesh**. AI generators tend to output one watertight shell. Options: (a) use a generator's part-segmentation feature (Tripo segmentation, Hunyuan3D-Part), (b) generate the insert and holder as **separate generations**, or (c) best for this project: **keep the AI holder and replace the insert (and possibly the clamp screw) with procedural geometry** built in code to ISO dimensions. Remember to pass `--join false` to gltf-transform so separate meshes are not merged.

### Cited Findings
- `gltf-transform optimize` (v4.5.1, verified locally) joins meshes by default (`--join true`, `--join-meshes true`, `--join-named true`, `--flatten true`). Simplification uses meshoptimizer with `--simplify-ratio` (target fraction of vertices) and `--simplify-error`. `--weld` is required for simplification. `--compress` takes draco/meshopt/quantize. `--texture-compress` takes ktx2/webp/avif — local CLI help; [glTF-Transform GitHub](https://github.com/donmccurdy/glTF-Transform) (individual commands also exist: `draco`, `meshopt`, `resize`, `webp`, `uastc`/`etc1s`, `weld`, `simplify`, `center`, `dedup`, `prune`).
- Meshy 6 outputs are "watertight" — [Toolworthy Meshy v6](https://www.toolworthy.ai/tool/meshy-ai-v6); Hunyuan claims "watertight meshes" — [3D AI Studio Hunyuan 3.1](https://www.3daistudio.com/Models/Hunyuan3D-3-1).
- **Hunyuan3D-Part** (Sept 2025): **P3-SAM** performs native 3D part segmentation and "can handle any input mesh." **X-Part** regenerates complete, structure-coherent parts from bounding boxes; the open release is a "light version," and the full version is in Hunyuan3D Studio. HF Space demo available — [Hunyuan3D-Part GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Part).
- Tripo offers model segmentation into semantic parts with "Part Completion" filling geometry exposed by each cut — [Tripo blog: auto part segmentation](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/ai-3d-auto-part-segmentation). CSM Cube previously offered image-to-parts (up to 30 parts) — [CSM on X](https://x.com/CSM_ai/status/1947821170377589195) (service status uncertain).
- Tripo H3.1 shows "clearer separation across layered components" vs Meshy 7 (vendor claim) — [Tripo H3.1 vs Meshy 7](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/tripo-h3-1-vs-meshy-7).

### Inferences
- **Recommended pipeline (developer, Linux):**
  1. Inspect: `npx @gltf-transform/cli inspect in.glb` (triangle count, texture sizes).
  2. Reorient/scale/pivot: a small Blender headless script (`blender -b -P fix.py`) or trimesh. Rotate so the shank axis is +X or +Z, scale so length = real mm/1000 (m), and set the origin to the bounding-box center (or the shank axis for nicer rotation). Blender availability in the container is not confirmed (`which blender` returned nothing); trimesh plus gltf-transform `center` can do most of this without Blender.
  3. Optimize: `npx @gltf-transform/cli optimize in.glb out.glb --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp --texture-size 2048 --simplify-ratio 0.3 --simplify-error 0.001 --join false` (tune the ratio to reach ~30–80k triangles; keep `--join false` if parts must animate separately). Remember to register `MeshoptDecoder` (or `DRACOLoader` for draco) on three.js `GLTFLoader`.
  4. Materials: in three.js traverse the scene and replace the AI material with `MeshPhysicalMaterial({ metalness: 1, roughness: 0.25–0.35, color: dark steel ~#3a3d42 })` for the holder, plus a separate coloured carbide/coating material for the insert (e.g., gold TiN ~#c9a24a or dark AlTiN ~#2b2a33, roughness ~0.3–0.4). Optionally keep only the AI **normal map** (if any) for micro-detail. Metals need an environment map (`PMREMGenerator` + `RoomEnvironment` or a studio HDRI) or they render nearly black. AI-baked albedo usually contains fake reflections and lighting that look wrong when the model rotates, which is why replacement is advisable.
  5. Splitting: if the generator returns separate meshes/nodes (segmentation or multi-part export), keep them. If it is one shell, try `bpy.ops.mesh.separate(type='LOOSE')` (works only when the insert is a disconnected shell) or trimesh `mesh.split()`. Otherwise cut by plane/bounding box, which leaves holes unless capped, or segment with Hunyuan3D-Part P3-SAM (GPU needed; HF Space).
  6. **Most robust "exploded" design:** generate the holder **without the insert** (prompt: "empty insert pocket, no insert") or delete the insert region, then add a **procedural insert** built in three.js. CNMG 120408 per ISO 1832 is an 80° rhombus, 12.7 mm inscribed circle, 4.76 mm thick, 0.8 mm nose radius, with a center hole (from domain knowledge; dimensions not verified in this research). Add a procedural clamp screw too. These parts can then fly out and back on scroll with perfect geometry and their own materials.
- Text/branding (e.g., "AKSOY" or the part number on the shank): add as a decal texture or a `DecalGeometry` in three.js, not via AI.

### Inferences
- **Manufacturer CAD quality:** a "detailed" STEP of a PCLNR holder + CNMG insert + clamp from ToolsUnited/ISCAR/Korloy is dimensionally exact and already split into separate solids (holder, insert, shim, clamp, screw). That makes the exploded animation trivial and gives clean, crisp metal surfaces once tessellated (cascadio tolerance e.g. linear 0.01–0.05 mm in model units). It is likely superior to any AI output. "Light/simplified" STEP versions may omit chip-breaker and clamp details. Prefer "detailed."
- **Conversion pipeline (container):** `pip install cascadio trimesh` → `cascadio.step_to_glb(...)` → gltf-transform `optimize --join false` → assign materials in three.js. Whether cascadio preserves STEP assembly structure as separate glTF nodes was not confirmed from the README; test on a real file. If it does not, convert each part's STEP separately.
- **Licensing:** manufacturer models carry the manufacturer's design and often its branding. Sandvik explicitly reserves commercial use. ISCAR, Korloy and Gühring terms were not read. Before using any brand's CAD as the homepage hero, Aksoy should ask its supplier/brand (as an authorized dealer) for written permission, or use a **generic/unbranded** model (AI or procedural). A model of a generic ISO PCLNR shape without logos is lower risk than a recognizable branded geometry.
- **Text-to-CAD** (Zoo/Adam) can produce simple prismatic shapes (a shank, a rhombic insert with a hole) as STEP. That is effectively procedural modeling with an LLM, but less controllable than writing the geometry directly. Backflip "photo to CAD" could turn a product photo into STEP but targets functional parts, so photoreal fidelity is unknown.
- **Procedural modeling (developer):** best for the end mill (4-flute profile swept along a helix with twist via vertex rotation proportional to axial position, plus a chamfered cylindrical shank and corner radii) and inserts (extruded rhombus/square with a bevel, a center hole via `three-bvh-csg` or a shape hole). A simplified holder is also possible (box shank + `ExtrudeGeometry` head with bevels + CSG pocket). Code-built models are tiny (KB), perfectly crisp, parametric, and free of licensing issues.

## Q7. Final recommendation: which product + which tool + step-by-step workflow for a non-technical Turkish user, with fallback

### Takeaway
**Product:** the **PCLNR external turning holder with a CNMG insert and top clamp** (generic, unbranded, right-hand). **Tool:** do a free-preview bake-off of one clean reference image in **Hyper3D Rodin Gen-2.5** (free previews; pay ~US$1.50/credit only to download; commercial rights on all tiers per third-party sources), **Hunyuan 3D global** (20 free/day; check commercial terms) and **Meshy 7.1 / Tripo H3.1** (free to preview; paid plan needed for commercial or attribution-free use). ### Inferences
- **Step-by-step for the user (can be translated to Turkish for them):**
  1. **Make the reference image.** Preferably photograph a real PCLNR 2020/2525 holder with a CNMG insert on white paper, in diffuse light, at a three-quarter view from slightly above, with the whole tool in frame (phone at max resolution, no zoom distortion: step back and crop). Or open Gemini (Nano Banana) / ChatGPT image and paste the reference-image prompt from Q3. Then ask: "remove all text and logos, make the background pure white, reduce reflections, keep the exact shape." Save as PNG ≥ 1024 px (ideally 2048 px).
  2. *(Optional, for multi-view tools)* In the same chat ask for "same object, exact side view," "exact top view," "exact front view." Keep only views that match perfectly.
  3. **Rodin (hyper3d.ai):** sign up → Image-to-3D → upload the PNG → Gen-2.5 → material **PBR**, mesh **Triangle/Raw**, highest quality tier affordable, symmetry off → generate (free preview) → rotate and inspect the insert, clamp and edges → regenerate 2–3 times if needed (free).
  4. **Hunyuan 3D global (3d.hunyuanglobal.com):** same image (or up to 8 views) → generate → inspect (free 20/day).
  5. **Meshy / Tripo:** same image → generate on the free tier just to *compare previews* (Meshy 7.1 image-to-3D; Tripo H3.1/v3.x). Don't use free downloads commercially (Meshy needs credit; Tripo free is non-commercial).

  7. **Download** the winner as **GLB, PBR, 2K textures, highest-detail triangle mesh** (pay the Rodin download, or take one month of Meshy Pro / Tripo Pro to download commercially). Also download the highest-poly or untextured version if offered. Send the files plus the reference image(s) to the developer.
  8. *(Nice-to-have)* Also generate **the holder with an empty insert pocket** ("no insert, empty pocket") as a second model. The developer adds a perfect procedural insert for the exploded scroll animation.
- **Developer then:** reorients, scales and pivots the model; simplifies to ~30–80k triangles; compresses (meshopt/WebP); replaces materials with procedural steel/carbide `MeshPhysicalMaterial` + environment map; adds a procedural CNMG insert and screw; and adds an "AKSOY" decal if desired.
