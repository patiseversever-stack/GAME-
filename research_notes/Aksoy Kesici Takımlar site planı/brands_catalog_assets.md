## 1. Brand identification and profiles (including the informal spellings)

### Takeaway
Five of the eight names resolve with medium to high confidence: Iscar, Tungaloy, Gühring, Korloy and Kyocera. "descar" is almost certainly DESKAR, a Chinese insert brand made by Lifeng Precision Tools in Zhejiang. SANT is Zhuzhou Sant Cutting Tools Co., Ltd. in Hunan (medium-high confidence). "kyoto" is almost certainly Kyocera, since no carbide-tool brand called "Kyoto" turned up. **"wunher" is still unidentified.** No cutting-tool brand called "Wunher" or "Wunder" exists in search results. The best candidates are Wohlhaupter, Walter, WNT and Würth, all low confidence, so ask the client.

ISCAR, Tungaloy, Gühring, KORLOY, Ceratizit and Kyocera (via a joint venture) all have **official Turkish subsidiaries**, each with its own dealer network. DESKAR is sold in Turkey mainly through online stores and marketplaces (deskarturkiye.com, teknikdukkanshop.com, N11, Hepsiburada, Amazon TR) at roughly 55–150 TL per insert. No Turkish importer was found for SANT.

**Legal**
- **SMK m.7/5 (descriptive/fair use):** the trademark owner cannot stop third parties from using the mark honestly and in the normal course of trade:
  - (a) to state their own name or address;
  - (b) to describe the type, quality, quantity, intended purpose, value, origin or other characteristics of goods;
  - (c) to indicate intended purpose where necessary, especially for accessories, spare parts or equivalent parts.

  Court practice requires that the use causes no confusion, does not exploit the mark's reputation, and does not present the product as if it were the original or official channel. — [Av. Zeki Emre Kurt – SMK m.7/5](https://www.emrekurt.av.tr/blogs/2025/07/21/marka-haklari-nerede-biter-smk-m-7-5-marka-hakkinin-sinirlari/); [SMK m.7 text](http://www.sinaimulkiyetkanunu.com/sinai-mulkiyet-kanunu-madde-7/); [Fırat Gültekin – markanın internette dürüst kullanımı](https://firatgultekin.com.tr/markanin-internette-durust-kullanimi/)
- **SMK m.152 (exhaustion, "hakkın tükenmesi"):** once protected products are put on the market by the right holder or with its consent, later acts involving those products fall outside the right. The owner can still act if products are altered or impaired (m.152/2). Exclusive-distributor contracts cannot stop third parties from reselling such goods. — [Av. Emre Kurt – SMK 152](https://www.emrekurt.av.tr/blogs/2024/03/02/sinai-mulkiyet-kanunusmk-152-hakkin-tuketilmesi/); [Türk Hukuk Sitesi – SMK m.152](https://www.turkhukuksitesi.com/mevzuat.php?mid=13564); [Aksan – tek yetkili satıcılık ve e-ticaret](https://aksan.av.tr/blog/tek-yetkili-saticilik-sozlesmelerinin-marka-hukuku-kapsaminda-elektronik-ticarete-etkisi); [Hukuk Defterleri – uluslararası tükenme](https://hukukdefterleri.com/6769-sayili-sinai-mulkiyet-yasasi-ve-uluslararasi-tukenme-ilkesi/)
- **TTK m.55/1-a-2 (unfair competition):** false or misleading statements about one's business or business relations are unfair competition. Falsely presenting oneself as a domestic or foreign company's representative, its authorized distributor in Turkey, or an authorized service is specifically cited as unfair competition, and this applies to websites and online marketing. — [KUTEL Hukuk – Yetkili satıcı olmadığı halde yapılan tanıtımlar ve haksız rekabet](https://www.kutelhukuk.com/blog/yetkili-satici-olmadigi-halde-bu-yonde-yapilan-tanitimlar-ve-haksiz-rekabet); [Fatih Aras – TTK m.55](https://www.fatiharas.com/ttk-madde-55-haksiz-rekabet-halleri/)
- **SMK m.30 (criminal trademark infringement):** producing, offering for sale, selling, importing or exporting goods that infringe a trademark, or commercially buying, holding, transporting or storing them, is punishable by 1–3 years' imprisonment and a judicial fine of up to 20,000 days. Sector merchants are expected to act as "prudent merchants", so a claim of not knowing the goods were counterfeit is hard to sustain. — [Anayasa Mahkemesi 2018/32](https://www.anayasa.gov.tr/Kararlar/GenelKurul/Basvuru_Karari/2018-32.pdf); [Şengün – Marka hakkına tecavüz suçu](https://sengunhukukyayinlari.com/marka-hakkina-tecavuz-sucu/); [YSA Hukuk](https://ysahukuk.com.tr/makaleler/marka-hakkina-tecavuz-ve-cezai-hukumler)
